## App Description (if updating)

```
Say It Anyway: Grief Journal

A private, safe space to stay connected with loved ones who have passed away.

COMPLETELY PRIVATE
• All data stored locally on your device
• No cloud storage or syncing
• No accounts or login required
• Your messages stay between you and your loved ones

MEANINGFUL FEATURES
• Create profiles for loved ones with photos and memories
• Write text messages or record audio messages
• Speech-to-text transcription for audio messages
• Hide messages without deleting them
• Beautiful, calming themes to personalize your experience
• Custom backgrounds with your own photos

SUPPORT WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Context-aware mental health screening
• Immediate access to crisis resources
• Location-based support for 28+ countries worldwide
• Crisis hotlines, text lines, and emergency contacts
• Compassionate, non-clinical language

DESIGNED FOR GRIEF
• Mobile-first, one-hand friendly interface
• Calm, soothing color themes
• Accessible design for all users
• No pressure, no judgment, just space to express yourself

Grief is personal. Your journal should be too.

Say It Anyway provides a private, offline space to process grief, share thoughts, and maintain connections with those you've lost. Whether you need to share your day, express difficult emotions, or simply say "I miss you," this app is here for you.

SUPPORT RESOURCES
If you're in crisis, the app provides immediate access to mental health resources including:
• 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US)
• Samaritans (UK)
• Crisis Services Canada
• Lifeline (Australia)
• And resources for 28+ countries worldwide

Your grief journey is unique. Say It Anyway is here to support you, privately and compassionately, every step of the way.
```

### Notes for Reviewer

```
Thank you for reviewing Say It Anyway: Grief Journal v1.1.0.

This is an UPDATE to the existing app (v1.0.0 → v1.1.0).

KEY FEATURES TO TEST:

1. LOCATION-BASED SUPPORT RESOURCES
   - Open Settings → scroll to "Support Resources Region"
   - Select different countries (US, UK, Canada, etc.)
   - Tap the "!!!" button on home screen
   - Verify support resources change based on selected region
   - Test phone/text/web links (they will open respective apps)

2. BACKGROUND CUSTOMIZATION
   - Open Settings → "Background Scene"
   - Select different scenes (Ocean, Forest, Mountains, etc.)
   - Try "Custom Photo" and upload a photo
   - Adjust transparency slider
   - Background should appear on all screens

3. MENTAL HEALTH SCREENING
   - Create a recipient (tap + button)
   - Create a message with concerning text like "I want to hurt myself"
   - After saving, support resources screen should appear automatically
   - This is a safety feature, not a bug

4. AUDIO RECORDING
   - Create a message and tap microphone icon
   - Grant microphone permission when prompted
   - Record a short message
   - Audio should play back and show transcription

PRIVACY NOTES:
- All data stored locally on device
- No network requests (except for opening external support links)
- No analytics or tracking
- No user accounts or authentication

UPGRADE NOTES:
- Users upgrading from v1.0.0 will have their data automatically migrated
- No user action required for migration
- All existing recipients and messages will be preserved

The app is designed to provide a private, safe space for grief journaling with immediate access to mental health support when needed.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
```
