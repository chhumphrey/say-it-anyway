# Say It Anyway: Grief Journal

**Say It Anyway: Grief Journal** is a private grief journal designed for the words that never got spoken — whether you write them down or say them out loud.

When someone is gone — or when a moment has passed — there are often thoughts, feelings, and questions that remain. This app gives you a quiet place to write or record spoken notes, without pressure, judgment, or an audience.

There are no accounts to create.
No timelines to maintain.
No expectations about how grief should look.

Just you, your thoughts, and the freedom to say what you need to say — anyway.

## Why this app exists
Grief doesn’t follow a schedule, and it doesn’t always come with clear answers. Sometimes writing helps. Sometimes speaking feels easier. Sometimes you need both.

This app was created to give you a private space to express what’s inside — in the way that feels right in the moment.

## Features
- Write journal entries or record spoken notes
- Simple, distraction-free experience
- Entries stored locally on your device
- No sign-ups, ads, or social sharing
- Designed for privacy and emotional safety
- Works offline

## Who it’s for
- Anyone grieving a loss
- Anyone processing difficult emotions
- Anyone who needs a private space to write or speak honestly

**This is not therapy.\
It’s not a productivity tool.\
It’s a place to speak freely — or write quietly — and keep going, one entry at a time.**
